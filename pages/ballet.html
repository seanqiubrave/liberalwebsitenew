Royal Academy of Dance

A globally respected pathway, made for every dancer

The RAD examination pathway is designed to make quality dance education accessible to students everywhere. It recognises learners' progress at every stage while helping them refine their technique and artistry over time. Open to all ages and skill levels — beginners, returners, and serious students alike.

RAD Graded Syllabus

Your child's ballet journey

Three progressive stages, each carefully crafted to build confidence, technique, and a lifelong love of dance.

1
Ages 2.5–5

Pre-Ballet

Early classes that nurture creativity, coordination, and fundamental physical skills — preparing young dancers for formal ballet training at higher levels. Playful, imaginative, and gentle.

2
Ages 5–7

Pre-Primary & Primary in Dance

Foundational stages that build confidence, musicality, and essential ballet movements in a structured yet engaging environment. The first steps into formal RAD examinations.

3
Ages 7+

Grades 1–8

The graded examinations within the RAD syllabus span eight progressive levels — covering three core dance disciplines that together form a complete classical training.

Grades 1–8 · Three Disciplines

Three dance forms, one beautiful art

At graded levels, students train across three complementary disciplines that together form the heart of the RAD syllabus.

Ballet
Core Discipline

Classical Ballet

A highly technical and widely practiced dance form that forms the core of the RAD syllabus. It provides the essential foundation for every graded level — precision, posture, line, and elegance.

Free
Expressive

Free Movement

An expressive style drawing from Natural Movement, Contemporary, and Classical Greek dance. Often involves improvisation inspired by music or creative prompts — a beautiful counterbalance to classical technique.

Folk
Cultural

Character Dance

Stylised interpretations of traditional European folk dances — including Hungarian, Russian, and Polish styles. An important component of the classical ballet repertoire that brings storytelling and cultural richness to training.
