The Journey

How do vocal lessons progress?

Vocal students can follow the ABRSM Singing or Trinity pathway depending on repertoire taste — classical, musical theatre or pop. Our ABRSM guide covers how the graded system works.

  1. Foundations. Pitch-matching, breath support, posture and simple songs in a comfortable range.
  2. First exams. ABRSM Singing or Trinity entry grades: prepared songs, technical work and aural, entered when ready.
  3. Developing voice. Range extension, dynamics, diction in English and Mandarin, and expressive phrasing.
  4. Advanced grades. Grades 6–8 repertoire, performance interpretation and recital-standard preparation.

FAQ

Vocal lessons in Jurong West — parents ask us

Where are your vocal lessons in Jurong West held?
Vocal lessons are held at Liberal Music & Arts School's Jurong West studio at Blk 492 Jurong West Street 41, #01-10, Singapore 640492, near Lakeside MRT, with HDB parking at the block.
What age can my child start singing lessons?
Most children start vocal lessons around age 5 to 6, once they can match pitch and follow simple melodies. Our teachers assess this gently during the free trial lesson.
Do you teach singing in Mandarin as well as English?
Yes. Repertoire spans English and Mandarin across pop, folk and classical styles, matched to each student's taste and any upcoming school or CCA performances.
Can vocal students take graded exams?
Yes — students can follow the ABRSM Singing or Trinity pathway to Grade 8 depending on repertoire preference. Teachers recommend the board that fits each voice and style.
Is it too late for teens or adults to start vocal lessons?
Not at all. Voices can be trained at any age, and teen and adult students often progress quickly because motivation and focus are already there.
