Our Story

Singapore's Most-Loved
Music School for Families

Liberal Music & Arts School, established in 2009, is Singapore's premier private institution offering comprehensive and professional courses for enthusiasts of all ages and skill levels.

Our extensive curriculum includes music courses covering Western and Chinese instruments, art courses focused on creative development, professional skill training and Direct School Admission (DSA) preparation, as well as dance courses ranging from ballet and hip hop to traditional Chinese dance.

World-class faculty comprising highly qualified instructors from Russia, China, Ukraine, Canada and beyond.

Four strategically located branches across Jurong West, Bukit Batok, Tengah, and Tampines.

Over 20,000 students taught — with graduates progressing to SOTA, NAFA, and LASALLE College of the Arts.

Our Philosophy

What Makes Liberal Different?

At Liberal Music & Arts School, our mission is to inspire, educate, and nurture a lifelong passion for music, art and dance. We believe in fostering a supportive environment where students can explore their artistic potential, foster creativity and lay a solid foundation for future success. Upholding the rich educational tradition of Liberal Arts schools, we aspire for every student to have a vibrant and promising future.

Confidence First

We build courage and self-belief before exam grades. Children learn to love performing because they feel safe to try, fail, and try again.

Certified Excellence

Every teacher holds internationally recognised qualifications — ABRSM, Trinity, Berklee, or the Central Conservatory of Music. No exceptions.

Warm & Safe

Our studios are fully supervised, child-safe environments. Parents trust us because we treat every child like our own.

Holistic Growth

Beyond music — we develop focus, discipline, emotional regulation, and a lifelong love of the arts that benefits children in every area of life.

Proven Results

200+ ABRSM Distinctions. 50+ competition awards. Many alumni now study at SOTA, NAFA, and LASALLE — the results speak for themselves.

Community Rooted

5 branches across Singapore — Tengah, Tampines, Jurong West, Le Quest, and Jurong Point — always a studio close to your family.

Principal & Founder

Ms. Cecily

Erhu · Chinese Instruments · Music Theory

Cecily founded Liberal Music & Arts School in 2009 with a vision to create Singapore's most trusted music school. A world-class Erhu specialist trained at the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts (Bachelor's, Erhu major, Piano minor) and holding a Master's in Music Education from Central China Normal University — with over 20 years of teaching experience spanning Singapore, Macao, and Hong Kong. She has performed before China's former Presidents Jiang Zemin and Hu Jintao, and toured internationally across Europe and Asia.
