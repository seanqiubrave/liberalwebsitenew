About Us

Singapore's Leading Private Music Academy

Liberal Music & Arts School, established in 2009, is one of Singapore's leading private music academies offering structured and professional courses for all ages and skill levels. Our programmes include piano, violin, drums, guitar, ukulele, vocals and Chinese instruments, taught by highly qualified international instructors from Russia, China, Ukraine and Canada.

With over 20,000 students served across our four locations in Jurong West, Bukit Batok, Tengah and Tampines, we have built a strong reputation for excellence. Many of our students have progressed to top institutions such as SOTA, NAFA and LASALLE College of the Arts.
