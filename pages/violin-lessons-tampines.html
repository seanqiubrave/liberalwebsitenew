The Journey

How do violin lessons progress?

Violin at Liberal follows the ABRSM strings pathway to Grade 8 — the ladder and Grade 5 Theory rule are in our ABRSM guide.

  1. Foundations. Posture, bow hold, open strings and first finger patterns.
  2. Initial–Grade 2. First ABRSM exams with simple repertoire, scales and aural.
  3. Grades 3–5. Shifting, early vibrato, richer repertoire; theory toward the Grade 5 gateway.
  4. Grades 6–8. Advanced positions, expressive playing, full mock exams.
