Music Courses

Individual lessons tailored to your child's pace, interest, and goals — from casual exploration to ABRSM certification.

🎹
Most Popular
Piano Lessons
All Levels ABRSM

Our most sought-after programme. Students learn classical and contemporary piano with structured lesson plans, building solid technique and preparing for ABRSM graded exams.
🎻
Violin Lessons
All Levels ABRSM

From posture and bow technique to performing pieces with confidence — our violin lessons nurture discipline, listening skills, and a lifelong love of strings.
🎸
Guitar Lessons
Classical Pop

Acoustic, classical, and pop styles taught for all ages. Students learn chords, strumming patterns, and finger-picking — perfect for those who love contemporary music.
🪗
Ukulele Lessons
Beginner Friendly

A wonderful first instrument! Ukulele lessons are fun, accessible, and build early musical confidence — ideal for young children starting their music journey.
🥁
Drum Lessons
Rhythm All Genres

Build incredible rhythm, coordination, and musical timing. Our drum lessons cover everything from basic beats to complex patterns — taught in a high-energy, structured environment.
🎤
Vocal Lessons
Technique Performance

Develop breath control, vocal range, and stage presence. Whether your child dreams of performing or simply loves to sing, our vocal coaches nurture every voice with care.
🎵
Music for Kids
Early Childhood

A playful music exploration programme for our youngest learners. Through songs, movement, and instruments, toddlers develop listening, coordination, and early music foundations.
🪘
Chinese Instruments
Erhu Guzheng Guqin

Connect with cultural heritage through traditional Chinese instruments. Our specialist instructors teach Erhu, Guzheng, and Guqin with authentic technique and deep musical tradition.
📝
Music Theory
ABRSM Trinity

Understand the language of music. Our theory classes prepare students for graded music theory examinations — a vital complement to practical instrument lessons.
👂
Aural Training
Ear Training

Train your ear to identify rhythms, intervals, and melodies by listening. Aural skills are essential for exam success and becoming a well-rounded, instinctive musician.
