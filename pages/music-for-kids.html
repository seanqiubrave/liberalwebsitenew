Our Approach

Where Music Begins.
Through Play, Movement, and Joy.

Our Music for Kids program is thoughtfully designed to introduce young children to the world of music in a fun, creative and engaging way. Specially tailored for children aged 3 to 5, this program blends play-based learning with foundational music education — creating a joyful environment where children can freely express themselves and grow through music.

Using the Orff Approach, children learn music in a way that is both natural and enjoyable. The Orff Method engages both mind and body through a combination of singing, movement, acting and hands-on percussion playing — with instruments such as xylophones, bells, castanets, tambourines, gongs and conga drums.

Through interactive lessons presented with an element of play, children explore key musical concepts such as rhythm, pitch, melody, and harmony — guided by caring and experienced instructors.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions parents ask before enrolling their little ones.

Is my 3-year-old too young for music classes?
Not at all! Our Music for Kids program is specifically designed for 3 to 5-year-olds. We use the play-based Orff Method, which perfectly matches their developmental stage, attention span, and natural energy.
Do parents need to accompany the child during the lesson?
For the trial class, parents are welcome to sit in. For regular classes, we encourage independence, so children attend the class with the teacher while parents relax in our waiting area.
What instruments will they play?
Children will get hands-on experience with age-appropriate Orff percussion instruments. This includes xylophones, glockenspiels, tambourines, castanets, bells, and conga drums.
