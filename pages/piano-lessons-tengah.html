The Journey

How do piano lessons progress?

Piano at Tengah follows the ABRSM pathway from Initial Grade to Grade 8. Our ABRSM Singapore guide covers the grades, pass marks and the Grade 5 Theory rule.

  1. Foundations. Posture, hand shape, reading both clefs and first pieces — with musicianship games for our youngest starters.
  2. Initial–Grade 2. First ABRSM exams: three pieces, scales, sight-reading and aural, entered only when genuinely ready.
  3. Grades 3–5. Repertoire deepens; Music Theory begins in parallel so the Grade 5 gateway is banked early.
  4. Grades 6–8. Advanced repertoire, mock assessments to exam standard, and recital stage-time before the real day.

Piano lessons in Tengah — parents ask us

Where are your piano lessons in Tengah held?
Piano lessons are held at Liberal Music & Arts School's headquarters studio at 127A Plantation Crescent, #01-381, Singapore 691127 — at Plantation Plaza, next to McDonald's, in the heart of Tengah's Plantation district.
How do I get to the Tengah studio?
The studio sits at Plantation Plaza on Plantation Crescent, reachable by bus or a short drive from anywhere in Tengah, Bukit Batok West and Choa Chu Kang. The upcoming Jurong Region Line (2028) will add a direct MRT connection.
What age can children start piano at Tengah?
Most children begin around age 3 to 4, and our Music for Kids programme welcomes little ones from 2.5 as a stepping stone. Readiness — finger strength, focus, interest — is assessed at the trial lesson.
Do you prepare students for ABRSM piano exams at this branch?
Yes. The Tengah studio runs the same ABRSM pathway as every Liberal branch, from Initial Grade to Grade 8, with theory scheduled in time for the Grade 5 gateway. School-wide, our students hold a 99% pass rate with 200+ Distinctions.
Are there weekend piano classes at Tengah?
Yes — Tengah opens Saturdays and Sundays from 9am to 7:30pm, and weekend-morning slots are the most popular with young families in the estate. Weekday classes run 1pm to 9pm.
