Step into the expressive world of violin playing with a course that blends technical discipline with musical creativity. Whether pursuing formal certification or learning for personal fulfillment, our violin program fosters patience, precision and artistry — supported by experienced instructors who tailor lessons to each student's pace and passion.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions parents ask before booking their first violin lesson.

What is the best age to start violin lessons?
We recommend starting violin lessons around age 4 to 5. At this age, children can comfortably hold a 1/16 or 1/10 size violin and begin developing the fine motor skills and pitch recognition required for string instruments.
Do I need to buy a violin before the first lesson?
No, you do not need to buy a violin immediately. We provide appropriately sized violins for students to use during their trial class. Our teachers will then measure your child's arm length and advise you on the exact fractional size (e.g., 1/4, 1/2) to purchase or rent.
How long does it take to prepare for an ABRSM violin exam?
Most students take about 9 to 12 months to prepare for a single ABRSM grade. Our structured curriculum covers repertoire, scales, sight-reading, and aural tests simultaneously, ensuring students are fully confident and contributing to our 99% pass rate.
