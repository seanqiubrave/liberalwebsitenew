Ms Kate

Piano Instructor · ABRSM · Classical & Contemporary

Since 2018 at Liberal
Master's Prokofiev Academy
ABRSM Certified
International Concert Pianist

Ms Kate is a classically trained concert pianist with a Master's degree from Ukraine's prestigious Music Academy named after Sergei Prokofiev. With rich international experience performing and teaching across Europe, the Middle East, and beyond, she brings world-class musicianship to every lesson at Liberal Music & Arts School.

Education Background

Master's Degree in Piano Performance, Accompaniment, Teaching & Chamber Music Music Academy named after Sergei Prokofiev, Ukraine
Bachelor's Degree in Piano Performance Myroslav Skoryk Music College, Ukraine

Achievements & Experience

Ms Kate has participated in prestigious music competitions and festivals across Turkey, Norway, England, and Australia, gaining international recognition as a skilled and versatile pianist. Her performance experience spans solo recitals and ensemble work at the highest levels of concert music worldwide.

She brings extensive experience as both a solo pianist and accompanist, having performed alongside singers and violinists in concerts and music competitions around the world. Her deep understanding of collaborative performance makes her especially effective at preparing students for examinations and stage performances.

Prior to joining Liberal, Ms Kate taught Music Theory and Piano at the International Centre for Music in Qatar, where she focused on nurturing individual talent and achieving high academic and performance standards in a multicultural setting.

Equally at home in classical and contemporary pop styles, Ms Kate has been part of the Liberal Music & Arts School family since 2018, guiding students from their very first notes through to distinction-level ABRSM examinations.
