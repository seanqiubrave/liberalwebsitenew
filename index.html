Why is Liberal the Most Trusted
Music School in Singapore?

Liberal Music & Arts School has been Singapore's #1 children's music school since 2009. With 5 branches, internationally certified teachers, a confidence-first teaching approach, and a 99% ABRSM exam pass rate, over 20,000 students have built lasting confidence through music.

Is Liberal Music School a safe environment for young children?

Yes. Liberal Music & Arts School maintains a fully supervised, child-safe environment across all 5 Singapore branches, with certified teachers trained in early childhood education for students as young as 4 years old.

Are the music teachers at Liberal School internationally certified?

Yes. All Liberal Music & Arts teachers hold qualifications from ABRSM, Trinity College London, Berklee Online, or the Central Conservatory of Music — ensuring consistent, professional-standard tuition for every student.

How many music school branches does Liberal have in Singapore?

Liberal Music & Arts School has 5 locations in Singapore: Tengah, Tampines, Jurong West, Le Quest Mall (Bukit Batok), and Jurong Point (opening soon) — covering central, east, and west Singapore.

Does music school help children with confidence and focus beyond music?

Yes. Research and our 15 years of experience show music education builds confidence, emotional regulation, and academic focus in children. Students perform in annual recitals, building real-world stage presence and self-belief.

FAQ

Parents Ask,
We Answer

Everything parents want to know about lessons, exams, locations, and getting started.

1

What is the best age to start music lessons?

Children as young as 4 years old can begin at Liberal. Our play-based programme builds rhythm, pitch, and listening skills. Research shows music before age 7 accelerates language development, focus, and emotional confidence.

2

Do you prepare students for ABRSM and Trinity exams?

Yes — over 200 ABRSM Distinctions and counting. All Piano, Violin, Guitar, and Erhu teachers are certified for Grades 1–8. Exam preparation is included in the regular curriculum at no extra cost.

3

Is the trial class really free?

Yes — completely free, no commitment required. Your child meets the teacher, tries the instrument, and we assess the best starting level. Parents are welcome to observe. No pressure to enrol.

Book Trial →
4

Do I need to buy an instrument first?

No — all instruments are provided in class. There is nothing to purchase before the first lesson. After 1–2 months our teachers can advise on budget-friendly starter options.

5

What's the difference between group and private lessons?

Private (1-to-1) gives undivided attention and faster progression. Group classes (2–4 students) are more affordable and great for younger beginners aged 4–7.

6

Where are your Singapore branches?

We have 5 locations: Tengah, Tampines, Jurong West, Le Quest Mall (Bukit Batok), and Jurong Point (opening soon). All branches offer Piano, Violin, Guitar, Drums, Erhu, Art, and Dance.
