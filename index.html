About Us

Singapore's Leading Private
Music Academy

Founded in 2009, Liberal Music & Arts School is one of Singapore's most established private music academies, offering structured and professional music programmes for all ages and skill levels. Our courses include piano, violin, drums, guitar, ukulele, vocals, and Chinese instruments — all taught by highly qualified international instructors from countries such as Russia, China, Ukraine, and Canada.

With over 20,000 students served across our 5 locations in Tengah, Tampines, Jurong West, Le Quest Mall, and Jurong Point (opening soon), we have earned a strong reputation for musical excellence. Many of our students have grown tremendously in both confidence and skill, with several going on to gain admission to top arts institutions such as SOTA, NAFA, and LASALLE.
