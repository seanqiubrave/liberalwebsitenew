Jurong West Music School

An established cornerstone of Liberal Music & Arts School in the West. Nurturing creativity, confidence, and musical excellence with our top-rated structured syllabuses since 2009.

Quick Facts: Liberal Music & Arts School Jurong West Branch is conveniently located at Blk 492 Jurong West Street 41, #01-10, Singapore 640492, positioned within a well-established residential hub. Jurong West Branch FAQ

Common questions about our Jurong West studio.

Where is Liberal Music School located in Jurong West?
Our Jurong West studio is located at Blk 492 Jurong West Street 41, #01-10, Singapore 640492. It is a well-established premium music studio positioned comfortably near Lakeside MRT station.
What programs are available at the Jurong West branch?
