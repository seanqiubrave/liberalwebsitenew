Frequently Asked Questions

Are your teachers certified?
Yes. All teachers hold internationally recognised qualifications — ABRSM, Trinity College London, Berklee Online, and more.
Do you teach traditional Chinese instruments?
Yes. We have specialists for Erhu, Guzheng, and Guqin alongside Western instruments.
What ages do your teachers work with?
Ages 4–16, with early childhood specialists for children as young as 4.
